# Vendor management

Recommendations are not a purchase, signature, renewal, or termination action. Separate them and
check safety, service continuity, data handling, budget, approval, and contract-review gates before
execution.

## Inputs and context

Collect requirement and volume, vendor scope, owner, service levels, support, references, financial
health, roadmap, total cost, contract dates, data and integrations, incident history, alternatives,
and exit effort.

## Workflow

### Recommendations

1. Define outcome, constraints, must-haves, service levels, failure cases, and evaluation weights before
   selecting a vendor.
2. Test the real operating path and failure modes, not only the demonstration path.
3. Assess supplier viability, support, customer relevance, total cost to operate, concentration, and
   exit feasibility.
4. Propose operational terms for export, support, price changes, notice, remedies, and transition;
   route legal language to qualified review.
5. Set one internal owner, performance cadence, renewal diary, issue log, and risk threshold.

### Actions

1. Confirm approval, contract status, security/privacy checks, implementation owner, continuity fallback,
   and migration plan before onboarding or renewal.
2. Validate integrations, data export, support paths, service measures, and recovery during a staged
   launch.
3. Review performance using independent evidence, log misses, and escalate before a renewal deadline.
4. If exiting, preserve data, test the replacement or fallback, communicate dependencies, and verify
   termination obligations are complete.

## Output / decision record

Return vendor assessment, total-cost view, service and risk baseline, recommendation, operational
terms, continuity and exit checks, approval status, owner, renewal dates, and follow-up actions.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not accept vendor-supplied evidence without corroboration for a critical service. Mark missing
export, support, financial, concentration, or contractual data; pause onboarding or renewal if no safe
fallback exists.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Choose from a product demo before defining the need.
- Renew a critical vendor without a performance and exit review.
- Treat a contractual promise as a tested continuity capability.
