# Procurement and sourcing

Recommendations do not authorize a purchase or supplier commitment. Separate them from actions and
check safety, continuity, data, implementation, budget, and legal-review gates before contacting or
selecting a supplier.

## Inputs and context

Collect desired outcome, volumes, must-have constraints, preferences, users, incumbent spend,
requirements, evaluation criteria, supplier alternatives, implementation effort, exit cost, and
critical dependencies.

## Workflow

### Recommendations

1. Describe the outcome and constraints before looking at products; label requirements versus preferences.
2. Define fair criteria, weights, evidence, commercial assumptions, and conflict controls in advance.
3. Compare credible alternatives, including sole-source limits, switching effort, concentration, and
   failure scenarios.
4. Calculate total cost through renewal and exit, including internal effort, integration, training,
   usage growth, and support.
5. Route data, security, service, and contract commitments to their accountable reviewers.

### Actions

1. Obtain approval for the sourcing method, budget, participants, evaluation record, and communications.
2. Issue the same material information to bidders and record questions and scoring consistently.
3. Validate the preferred option with references, failure tests, implementation plan, safety and
   continuity checks, and an exit path.
4. Preserve the decision record and send final terms to qualified contract review before signature.

## Output / decision record

Return requirements, evaluation method, options and scores, total-cost view, concentration findings,
recommendation, approvals, continuity checks, negotiation points, and implementation owner.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not fabricate competition or score against criteria added after responses. Mark unavailable evidence,
sole-source dependency, unpriced exit, and unresolved commitments; pause signature until owners review.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Write a requirement to fit a preferred product.
- Treat quoted price as total cost.
- Commit a critical dependency without an exit or continuity plan.
