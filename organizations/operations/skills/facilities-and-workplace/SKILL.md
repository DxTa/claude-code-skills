# Facilities and workplace

Keep workplace recommendations separate from physical actions. Before changing a site, lease, access
arrangement, or service, confirm accountable ownership, worker safety, accessibility, continuity,
communications, vendor obligations, and a reversible fallback where possible.

## Inputs and context

Collect attendance and room-use data, site constraints, lease terms, occupancy and safety records,
worker locations, accessibility needs, service failures, building dependencies, costs, and emergency
contacts.

## Workflow

### Recommendations

1. Size space from observed peaks, room demand, hybrid patterns, and customer or worker needs.
2. Model total commitment, exit and restoration obligations, service reliability, and alternative sites.
3. Review health, safety, accessibility, fire, evacuation, and inspection requirements with qualified
   local specialists where required.
4. Define workplace services, vendor ownership, incident thresholds, and continuity alternatives.

### Actions

1. Obtain approval and verify site safety, occupancy, access, insurance, and communications before work.
2. Pilot layout, booking, service, or hybrid-policy changes without blocking emergency routes or critical
   operations.
3. Record incident response, vendor performance, worker impact, and restoration or fallback status.
4. Escalate hazards immediately, preserve evidence, and suspend unsafe work until cleared.

## Output / decision record

Return utilization evidence, options and cost trade-offs, safety and continuity checks, applicable
professional-review questions, action owner, incident or change record, and review date.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not infer compliance from a policy or lease summary. Mark unknown obligations, occupancy limits,
accessibility gaps, and dependencies; use qualified inspection or legal review before a consequential
commitment.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Size a workplace from headcount alone.
- Block an emergency or continuity route for convenience.
- Treat an unverified site condition as safe.
