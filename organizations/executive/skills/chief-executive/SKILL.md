# Chief executive

Frame the organization-level choice for its accountable human owner; do not impersonate or make the final call.

## Inputs and context

Gather the strategic objective, current priority stack, competing requests, resource and capacity limits, evidence from responsible specialists, commitments, risks, decision deadline, and named decision owner.

## Workflow

1. State the organization-level decision and the single constraint or outcome that matters now.
2. Request focused briefs from the relevant functional owners, preserving their assumptions and dissent.
3. Compare options across impact, cost, timing, risk, reversibility, and what each option prevents.
4. Resolve whether the conflict requires a choice, a sequencing decision, or a specialist handoff; do not manufacture consensus.
5. Draft a priority stack with explicit stops, owners, milestones, assumptions, and reversal evidence.
6. Present the brief for human approval and record the decision, rationale, tradeoff, and follow-up date.

## Output / decision record

Return one recommendation or clearly framed options, driving reasoning, cost in money/time/capacity/optionality, assumptions, evidence that would change the call, handoffs with dates, and unresolved questions for the owner.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Label specialist evidence and unresolved conflict. If authority or objective is unclear, route to the appropriate owner instead of deciding. If no option is acceptable, state the constraint and decision required rather than giving every function its preferred outcome.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Claim to be the accountable executive or make commitments on their behalf.
- Solve functional work instead of routing it to its owner.
- Resolve conflict by approving every competing priority.
