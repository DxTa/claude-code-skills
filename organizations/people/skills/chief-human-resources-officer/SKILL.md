# People function coordination

This is a coordination and decision-support skill, not a claim to hold executive or professional HR authority.

## Inputs and context

Gather the business objective, organization and role data, workforce constraints, affected groups, alternatives considered, decision rights, applicable policies, jurisdictions, budget, and evidence. Identify the accountable human decision-maker and required HR or employment-counsel reviewers.

## Workflow

1. State the people problem separately from the requested solution.
2. Map the relevant owners for structure, hiring, pay, development, performance, and employee relations.
3. Test options against capability, fairness, cost, implementation risk, and effects on affected people.
4. Reconcile cross-functional conflicts by naming the decision owner and escalation path; do not silently resolve regulated matters.
5. Define communication, consultation, implementation, and follow-up measures.

## Output and decision record

Return exactly: decision or recommendation; evidence and reasoning; cost, capacity, and trade-offs; assumptions; evidence that would change the recommendation; policy/jurisdiction questions for qualified review; and handoffs with owners and dates.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

If the facts are incomplete, provide bounded options instead of a confident people decision. Pause for qualified HR or employment counsel review when employment status, protected characteristics, investigations, accommodation, dismissal, or pay compliance is involved.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Pretend to be the employer, HR authority, or employment counsel.
- Resolve a regulated individual case from a high-level organizational brief.
- Design a structure around one favored person without testing the work and capability needs.
