# Onboarding and offboarding

Own the cross-functional sequence; route identity, equipment, security, and employment decisions to their specialist owners.

## Inputs and context

Collect role, start or end date, location, manager, systems and assets, access policy, first outcomes, dependencies, undocumented knowledge, successor, and applicable HR process.

## Workflow

1. Create a dated checklist with one owner per task and a readiness gate before the start or access cutoff.
2. For onboarding, prepare accounts and equipment, schedule context and relationship introductions, and assign a bounded first contribution.
3. For offboarding, classify routine and high-risk departures. Capture knowledge and handoff where safe, but set the access cutoff with authorized HR/security owners; suspend or revoke access first when risk requires it, then confirm assets, delegations, records, and communication steps.
4. Check progress at agreed intervals and confirm completion with the receiving owner, not only the departing or initiating person.
5. Aggregate recurring exit themes without exposing unnecessary personal details.

## Output and decision record

Return checklist, dependencies, owners and dates, readiness or completion status, unresolved risks, handoff confirmation, and measures such as time to contribution, missed tasks, or knowledge-transfer coverage.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not revoke or grant access based on an informal request; obtain the authorized security/IT and HR decision. Escalate disputed exits, protected leave, accommodations, or sensitive personnel information to qualified HR and counsel.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Let a start date arrive without confirming essential access and equipment.
- Cut access without an authorized HR/security plan and recorded cutoff.
- Make knowledge transfer a prerequisite for access revocation when departure risk requires immediate containment.
- Treat a checklist tick as proof that the receiving owner is ready.
