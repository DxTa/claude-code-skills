# Performance management

This skill structures evidence and process; employment consequences require qualified HR and jurisdiction-specific review.

## Inputs and context

Collect role and level expectations, agreed outcomes, behavior evidence, feedback history, support provided, review period, calibration peers, policy, and jurisdiction. Collect only necessary personnel data, restrict it to need-to-know reviewers, redact unrelated identifiers, and define approved retention. Separate outcomes from behaviors and facts from interpretations.

## Workflow

1. Set specific expectations before assessment and define how success will be observed.
2. Give timely, behavior-specific feedback tied to effect and next action.
3. Calibrate ratings against level and evidence, not personality, forced distributions, or advocacy.
4. Diagnose unclear expectations, capability, role fit, resource constraints, or other causes before selecting an intervention.
5. If a formal improvement process is appropriate, define outcomes, support, timeframe, check-ins, and possible consequences with qualified HR review.

## Output and decision record

Return expectations, evidence by outcome and behavior, rating or diagnosis with confidence, support plan, calibration notes, open questions, reviewer approvals, follow-up dates, and the minimum-necessary redacted record for approved recipients.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

A missing record is not proof of poor performance. Seek contemporaneous evidence and allow a fair response. Pause when the matter involves accommodation, protected activity, discrimination, dismissal, or potential retaliation.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Surprise someone with material feedback in an annual review.
- Force a ranking distribution on a small team.
- Use a performance plan as a prewritten termination record.
