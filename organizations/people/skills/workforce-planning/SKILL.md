# Workforce planning

Produce scenarios for a human decision-maker; do not make employment or compensation determinations.

## Inputs and context

Collect strategic outcomes, current capability, workload and capacity, role demand, hiring lead times, ramp and absorption limits, attrition by segment, fully loaded costs, budget, contractors, and hard-to-reverse commitments.

## Workflow

1. Start with capabilities and outcomes, then test development, automation, contracting, deferral, and hiring as alternatives.
2. Model base, growth, and contraction cases with dates, dependencies, ramp, attrition, and cash impact.
3. Sequence enabling or scarce roles before dependent hires and test team absorption.
4. Examine regretted attrition and backfill lead time by team, level, tenure, and manager without exposing unnecessary personal data.
5. Record trigger thresholds, approval gates, communications, and a review cadence.

## Output and decision record

Return scenario table, capability gaps, hiring or non-hiring sequence, assumptions and confidence, cost and capacity trade-offs, trigger metrics, owners, and decision dates.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Use ranges when demand, attrition, or ramp is uncertain. Do not turn a forecast into a promise. Route role changes, individual selection, reductions, pay, protected status, or jurisdiction questions to qualified HR and counsel.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Plan from salary alone instead of fully loaded cost.
- Treat aggregate attrition as sufficient evidence.
- Hire faster than the team can absorb and support.
