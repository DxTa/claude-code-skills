# Benefits and leave

Use this skill for decision support, not for deciding what a policy or individual case legally permits.

## Inputs and context

Collect the minimum workforce profile, locations and jurisdictions, current plans, eligibility rules, utilization, renewal dates, budget, leave request facts, and the accountable HR owner. Minimize identifiers, restrict access to need-to-know reviewers, and identify the notice, consent, retention, and deletion basis for any personal data. Identify which facts are verified and which are assumptions.

## Workflow

1. Separate plan design, administration, communication, and individual-case handling.
2. Compare options by employee value, cost, utilization, operational load, and likely equity effects.
3. For leave, map notice, documentation, pay treatment, concurrent leave, accommodation signals, and return-to-work handoffs without interpreting the law.
4. Flag jurisdiction-specific, protected-status, disability, pregnancy, tax, or plan-compliance questions for qualified HR or employment counsel review.
5. Define owners, decision dates, employee communications, and a review measure such as utilization, renewal cost, processing time, or unresolved cases.

## Output and decision record

Return: recommendation or open decision; evidence and assumptions; option comparison; employee and cost impacts; jurisdiction/policy questions requiring review; owners and dates; measures to revisit after rollout or renewal; and a redacted, access-controlled handling note for personal data.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not infer eligibility from incomplete facts. Mark unknown plan terms and ask for the governing document. If a request may involve accommodation, protected leave, discrimination, or a dispute, pause the substantive recommendation and escalate to qualified reviewers.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Make an employment-law or benefits-compliance determination.
- Promise an individual an entitlement from incomplete policy facts.
- Hide employee cost shifts or unequal impacts in aggregate totals.
