# Employee relations

This skill organizes a fair process; it does not determine employment-law rights or outcomes.

## Inputs and context

Record the report, dates, people involved, policy context, immediate safety concerns, evidence sources, reporting lines, jurisdiction, and the desired process. Limit access to need-to-know participants and identify an independent process owner.

## Workflow

1. Acknowledge the report without promising absolute confidentiality, retaliation protection, or a result.
2. Triage immediate safety, preservation, interim measures, conflicts of interest, and counsel needs.
3. Define a written scope; gather records and hear the complainant and respondent fairly.
4. Separate factual findings from policy interpretation, discipline, or termination recommendations.
5. Document decisions, communication commitments, appeal or review routes, and anti-retaliation monitoring.

## Output and decision record

Return intake facts and gaps; scope and process owner; evidence plan; findings stated with confidence; unresolved questions; qualified-review handoffs; and next actions with owners and dates.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Preserve uncertainty rather than choosing a side from an allegation. Pause and escalate when the matter involves discrimination, harassment, retaliation, accommodation, dismissal, protected activity, threats, or conflicting legal obligations.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Promise confidentiality that the process cannot provide.
- Investigate a matter where the investigator has a reporting or personal conflict.
- Recommend termination or legal conclusions without qualified review.
