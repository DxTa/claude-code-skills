# App store optimization

Separate discovery from the listing's ability to convert a qualified visitor.

## Inputs and context

Collect platform, current title and fields, target audience and search terms, category, impression-to-view and view-to-install data, screenshots, icon, ratings and reviews, release history, localization, and test constraints.

## Workflow

1. Diagnose whether the bottleneck is search visibility, listing conversion, or post-install quality.
2. Map high-intent terms to platform-supported fields without duplicating terms or making unsupported claims.
3. Rework the first screenshot, caption, icon, and call to value around the user's outcome; show the real product at usable scale.
4. Improve review prompts after successful moments and respond to negative reviews with specific verified changes.
5. Choose one material variable per experiment, define a full weekly observation window, and segment by platform, locale, and traffic source.
6. Record the winner, guardrail metrics, and next listing or product action.

## Output / decision record

Return diagnosis, target term map, listing changes, asset rationale, review response plan, experiment hypothesis and duration, primary and guardrail metrics, and release decision.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not infer keyword or creative impact from a short or mixed-traffic window. Separate ranking, impression, view, install, retention, and crash effects. If reviews reveal a product defect, route it to the product owner rather than answering it with copy.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Optimize for irrelevant search terms or promise functionality the app does not provide.
- Change multiple major listing elements and claim one caused the lift.
- Prompt for a rating before the user experiences success.
