# Paid advertising

Set the economic boundary and measurement path before buying traffic.

## Inputs and context

Gather objective, audience and intent, offer and landing page, margin and payback ceiling, conversion definition, platform access, budget, naming convention, creative variants, downstream quality, read-by date, and kill threshold.

## Workflow

1. Verify an end-to-end test conversion and confirm the destination delivers the promised experience.
2. Set maximum acceptable acquisition cost from contribution economics and payback, not available budget.
3. Structure campaigns by meaningful intent or audience while preserving enough volume for learning; apply a consistent taxonomy.
4. Test distinct messages or creative angles with one main variable, aligned landing page promise, and fatigue monitoring.
5. Read performance in sequence: delivery, click, conversion, acquisition cost, retention or revenue quality.
6. Decide scale, iterate, hold, or stop against the predeclared threshold and observation window.

## Output / decision record

Return campaign brief, economics, audience and structure, naming scheme, tracking proof, creative hypotheses, budget and bid guardrails, funnel results, quality caveats, decision, and next read date.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not scale on a few conversions or use platform attribution as causal proof without a test. Investigate tracking, auction mix, landing-page mismatch, and creative fatigue before changing targeting. Pause spend when the kill threshold or consent requirement is breached.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Spend against broken tracking or a destination that cannot convert.
- Change multiple causal variables and claim a winner.
- Scale cheap conversions without checking whether customers retain or pay back.
