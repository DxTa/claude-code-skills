# Voice of customer

A feedback program is useful only when evidence reaches an owner and produces a visible response.

## Inputs and context

Collect source channels, sampling method, customer segments, support and churn data, survey questions and free text, interview notes, behavioral evidence, existing theme taxonomy, and product or service owners.

## Workflow

1. Inventory sources and state the bias of each: volume, depth, self-selection, timing, and missing silent customers.
2. Normalize feedback into stable problem themes while keeping the customer's wording and segment context.
3. Distinguish a customer's requested solution from the underlying problem.
4. Quantify each theme by affected customers, contact or churn rate, severity, revenue or segment exposure, and behavioral corroboration.
5. Assign a non-collecting owner and bring the ranked list to a recurring cross-functional review.
6. Record the action, communicate relevant changes back to customers and frontline teams, and measure whether the problem signal moved.

## Output / decision record

Return source and sampling notes, prioritized themes with counts and segments, representative evidence, underlying problem, named owner, proposed action, decision status, loop-closure message, and follow-up measure.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not treat CSAT, NPS, reviews, or one loud account as representative without segmentation and corroboration. Report missing or conflicting evidence. If a score changes, inspect question timing, response mix, and behavior before attributing it to experience.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Report a theme without its volume, segment, or evidence basis.
- Let a single customer dictate a broad change without checking prevalence.
- Collect feedback without a path to action and a return message.
