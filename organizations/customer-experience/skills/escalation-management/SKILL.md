# Escalation management

Treat an escalation as both an urgent customer problem and evidence that the normal path failed.

## Inputs and context

Gather the customer's blocked task, affected users, business deadline, workaround, account context, known facts, current commitments, internal responders, and communication channel. Record the time zone and next-update expectation.

## Workflow

1. Assess severity from customer impact, breadth, urgency, regulatory or contractual consequence, and workaround quality.
2. Assign one visible owner and a responder group; give the customer a clear contact and update cadence.
3. Acknowledge impact without guessing at cause, then communicate verified progress and next update time.
4. Brief any executive before participation with facts, actions, commitments, and promises they must not make.
5. Coordinate recovery around a concrete fix, customer validation, and a safe interim workaround.
6. Review the escalation: why normal handling failed, whether severity and communication were right, and what prevents recurrence.

## Output / decision record

Return severity and rationale, customer impact, owner and responders, verified facts versus hypotheses, update schedule, approved commitments, recovery steps, and prevention actions with owners.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Label cause as unknown until confirmed. If the promised cadence cannot be met, say so early and reset it. Escalate contractual, safety, privacy, security, or regulatory implications to the responsible specialists; preserve a timeline of decisions and communications.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Leave the customer to repeat the story while ownership changes.
- Offer an unverified explanation or an unapproved delivery promise.
- Close on an apology or credit without testing whether the underlying failure was addressed.
