# Project delivery

Use this for one bounded effort with an end; coordinate several related efforts with program management.

## Inputs and context

Collect outcome, sponsor, scope and exclusions, deliverables and acceptance tests, dependencies, estimates and assumptions, resources, dates, constraints, risks, and baseline.

## Workflow

1. Write the outcome, inclusions, exclusions, owner, acceptance evidence, and decision rights.
2. Decompose work until estimates are understandable; use ranges, confidence, and assumptions rather than a negotiated point promise.
3. Build and regularly recalculate dependencies, float, critical path, and project-level buffer.
4. Track completed deliverables and remaining work against the baseline; separate facts, forecasts, and blockers.
5. If late, diagnose scope, capacity, dependency, or estimate causes, present cut/extend/capacity choices, and re-baseline visibly once approved.

## Output and decision record

Return scope record, work breakdown, estimate ranges, schedule and critical path, progress evidence, risks, recovery options, assumptions, decision owner, and next review.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Use confidence ranges and make missing acceptance evidence visible. Do not treat percentage-complete reports as proof. Escalate changes to scope, funding, safety, or contractual commitments to the authorized owner.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Accept scope without written exclusions and acceptance evidence.
- Present a single-point estimate as a commitment.
- Add people to a late project without accounting for onboarding and coordination cost.
