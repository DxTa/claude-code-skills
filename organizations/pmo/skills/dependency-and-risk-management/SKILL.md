# Dependency and risk management

Focus on risks to a bounded delivery effort; do not substitute for enterprise-risk governance.

## Inputs and context

Collect scope, milestones, dependencies, owning teams, committed dates, assumptions, risk events, likelihood and impact scales, decision dates, authority limits, and available mitigations.

## Workflow

1. Record each dependency as a concrete deliverable, named owner, committed date, acceptance condition, and late consequence; confirm it with the owning team.
2. Trace high-impact dependencies one level further for transitive constraints and shared specialists.
3. Write risks as event, cause, and impact; size likelihood and impact separately and assign an owner able to act.
4. Choose avoid, reduce, transfer, or explicit acceptance; pair serious mitigations with prepared contingencies.
5. Review by exception and escalate with the decision required, options, authority, and decision date.

## Output and decision record

Return dependency and risk register, confidence and evidence, owner commitments, response and contingency, trigger, escalation, decision date, and next review.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Label unconfirmed dates as assumptions, not commitments. Use ranges where evidence is weak, and escalate authority or timing gaps before a risk materializes. Keep delivery risk separate from compliance or enterprise-risk conclusions.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Track a dependency as committed without the owning team's agreement.
- Assign a risk to someone who cannot change its exposure.
- Treat silent acceptance as an approved response.
