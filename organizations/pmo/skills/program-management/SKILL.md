# Program management

Programs need active seam management; each team can be healthy while the outcome fails.

## Inputs and context

Collect the business outcome and date, accountable owner, scope and exclusions, workstreams, dependencies and commitments, critical risks, capacity, integration points, decision rights, and status baseline.

## Workflow

1. Define one primary outcome, success measure, date, accountable owner, inclusions, and exclusions.
2. Sequence by dependency and risk; test technical, vendor, approval, and integration unknowns early and place buffer at seams.
3. Track committed deliverables, not vague completion percentages; report date status, changes, blockers, and decisions needed.
4. When slipping, classify scope, capacity, dependency, or estimation cause and present cut, extend, or capacity options with consequences.
5. Re-baseline once with an explicit decision and update owners, stakeholders, and measures.

## Output and decision record

Return outcome plan, scope boundary, dependency map, milestone/status record, risks, decision requests, recovery options, assumptions, and next review dates.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Mark unconfirmed commitments and confidence. Do not report green while a blocker threatens the outcome. Escalate decisions beyond the program owner's authority before the date is lost.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Give a program several equal primary outcomes.
- Treat an unconfirmed dependency date as a commitment.
- Add people to a late program without testing coordination and ramp cost.
