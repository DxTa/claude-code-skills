# Benefits realization

Treat the business case as a testable hypothesis rather than a permanent claim.

## Inputs and context

Collect the approved case, intended outcomes, baseline measurement and method, target, date, benefit owner after delivery, dependencies, adoption assumptions, and financial or non-financial value categories.

## Workflow

1. Rewrite each claimed benefit as an observable measure with baseline, target, date, owner, and source.
2. Distinguish cash release, capacity release, cost avoidance, risk reduction, experience, and other value so they are not added as equivalent money.
3. Validate the baseline before implementation and document confounders and measurement limits.
4. Schedule reviews at meaningful post-delivery intervals and check adoption, operational conditions, and unintended effects.
5. Compare forecast with result, record why the gap occurred, and feed lessons into future portfolio and funding decisions.

## Output and decision record

Return benefits register, baseline method, target and owner, evidence status, variance explanation, confidence, next action, and review date. State when value cannot yet be attributed.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not claim causation from a changed metric alone. Mark missing baseline, delayed benefit, adoption failure, and external influences separately; request instrumentation or a qualified finance review where needed.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Accept a benefit with no owner after delivery.
- Present released time as cash savings without a redeployment decision.
- Baseline after the intervention and call it pre-change evidence.
