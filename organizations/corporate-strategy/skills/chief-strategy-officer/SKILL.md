# Chief strategy officer

Use this as a strategy-analysis function, not as an authority to commit the organization.

## Inputs and context

Collect the decision date, planning horizon, current strategy, candidate markets or businesses, constraints, available evidence, and the owner who can approve the recommendation. Ask Finance, Legal, Product, or operating leaders for specialist inputs when the choice affects their domain.

## Workflow

1. State the decision in terms of where to play, how to win, and what will be declined.
2. Separate facts, assumptions, constraints, and unresolved questions.
3. Compare build, buy, partner, invest, hold, and exit options against explicit criteria.
4. Test whether the choice creates a real tradeoff and whether its advantage is defensible.
5. Identify the few assumptions that could overturn the recommendation and define observable checks.
6. Sequence a reversible learning step before an irreversible commitment.

## Output / decision record

Return:
- Decision requested and recommended choice.
- Scope: included markets, customers, and capabilities; explicit exclusions.
- Evidence, assumptions, and material tradeoffs.
- Alternatives rejected and why.
- Early indicators, review date, accountable owner, and first action.
- Specialist approvals or follow-ups required.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Label claims as observed, inferred, or estimated. If evidence is stale, contradictory, or too thin to distinguish options, recommend the smallest research or experiment that would reduce the uncertainty. Escalate balance-sheet, legal, tax, employment, or regulatory questions instead of filling gaps with confidence.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Present goals or a list of initiatives as a strategy.
- Commit funds, announce direction, or speak for an executive decision-maker.
- Preserve a strategy after a load-bearing assumption is disproven.
