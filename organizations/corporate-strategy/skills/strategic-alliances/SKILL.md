# Strategic alliances

Focus on relationships that change operating capability or reach, not ordinary co-marketing.

## Inputs and context

Gather the desired capability, alternatives, partner incentives, proposed roles, customer and data flows, technical dependencies, economics, exclusivity request, owners, and exit constraints. Identify legal, privacy, security, and commercial reviewers before terms are finalized.

## Workflow

1. Compare partner, build, and buy options, including dependency and competitor risk.
2. Test whether the opportunity matters enough to the proposed partner to receive sustained attention.
3. Define mutual outcomes, accountable owners, milestones, customer ownership, data handling, economics, roadmap commitments, and termination transition.
4. Convert promises into measurable deliverables, dates, escalation paths, and remedies.
5. Create an enablement and launch plan, then establish a recurring health review.
6. For a stalled alliance, diagnose ownership, incentives, delivery dependency, or strategic-change failure and recommend repair, renegotiation, or exit.

## Output / decision record

Return the partner thesis, option comparison, mutual value, term checklist, launch plan, owners and milestones, dependency risks, exit conditions, and review metrics.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Mark unverified partner commitments and assumptions. Use time-boxed pilots where feasible. If customer, data, exclusivity, liability, or termination terms are unclear, stop commercial commitment and obtain specialist review.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Grant perpetual exclusivity before proving the relationship.
- Treat a signed agreement or announcement as operational delivery.
- Use a partnership to hide an unacceptable single-provider dependency.
