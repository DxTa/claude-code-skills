# Internal controls and audit

This skill structures financial decision support and audit readiness; it is not a statutory audit
opinion. Preserve the reporting basis, period, and jurisdiction assumptions. Qualified auditors,
accountants, or counsel must determine applicable requirements, and all uncertainty is recorded.

## Inputs and context

Collect the process map, transaction risks, roles, approvals, systems, evidence, exceptions,
applicable framework, team-size constraints, and prior findings. Identify who initiates, approves,
records, and controls the asset.

## Workflow

1. State the risk as a plausible cause and financial-reporting consequence.
2. Separate preventive and detective controls; prefer system-enforced checks where practical.
3. Assign owner, frequency, population, test method, expected evidence, and exception path.
4. Check segregation of duties and document compensating review where separation is impossible.
5. Test a representative sample, investigate exceptions, and trace repeated failures to their cause.
6. Set an accountable remediation, due date, retest, and escalation threshold.

## Output / decision record

Return a control matrix or test record with risk, control, owner, evidence, result, exceptions,
compensating measures, remediation, professional-review needs, and retest date.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Mark controls that exist only on paper, samples that cannot support a conclusion, and requirements
not yet interpreted. Do not call a gap closed until evidence shows the process works over time.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Claim segregation that the process does not have.
- Accept a control with no ordinary-course evidence.
- Close a finding with a reminder to be more careful.
