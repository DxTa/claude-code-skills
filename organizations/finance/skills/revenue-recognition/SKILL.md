# Revenue recognition

This is accounting decision support, not an accounting conclusion. Preserve the contract version,
transaction facts, reporting period, jurisdiction, and applicable standard assumptions. A qualified
accountant or auditor must determine material or unusual treatment; flag every unresolved judgment.

## Inputs and context

Request the signed or proposed agreement, side letters, deliverables, acceptance terms, renewal and
termination rights, price components, payment timing, customer context, and applicable reporting
framework. Confirm whether implementation, support, or usage elements are distinct.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the contract facts and search for promises outside the main agreement.
2. List promised goods or services and assess which are separately identifiable.
3. Map consideration, variable amounts, financing effects, discounts, and allocation evidence.
4. Describe when control or service transfer is expected and what evidence supports that timing.
5. Review deferred balances by obligation and expected release pattern.
6. Compare the proposed treatment with the relevant professional interpretation before signature.

## Output / decision record

Return a fact inventory, obligation map, timing rationale, open judgments, proposed accounting path,
contract changes to consider, deferred-revenue implications, and named professional reviewer.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not infer treatment from invoice or cash dates. Escalate missing side letters, novel terms, weak
standalone-price evidence, jurisdiction conflicts, and material estimates to a qualified professional
before relying on the result.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- State a material accounting conclusion as a checklist result.
- Ignore promises made outside the principal contract.
- Allocate consideration merely because a quote uses that layout.
