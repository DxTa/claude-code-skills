# Budgeting and forecasting

Use this skill for financial decision support, not as an approval authority. Preserve the source,
period, and jurisdiction assumptions; have a qualified finance professional determine material
accounting or reporting positions, and flag uncertainty rather than filling gaps silently.

## Inputs and context

Collect the planning horizon, approved budget, latest actuals, departmental submissions, headcount
plan, timing conventions, and the owner of each assumption. Mark whether each number is a commitment,
a current estimate, or a scenario.

## Workflow

1. Establish one planning frame: currency, periods, scope, targets, and shared drivers.
2. Gather submissions in a common structure and reconcile them to actuals and prior versions.
3. Challenge material drivers by asking what creates the volume, rate, timing, and mix.
4. Separate funded commitments from forecast beliefs; record unfunded requests and trade-offs.
5. Reforecast on a fixed cadence and update only changed assumptions with a reason.
6. Explain material variances against a named baseline, separating volume, rate, timing, and mix.

## Output / decision record

Return a concise budget or forecast, an assumption register with owners and dates, a variance bridge,
known unfunded items, confidence limits, and the decision or approval still required. Include the
next review date and the evidence that would change the forecast.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Stop and request missing period, scope, or ownership data when figures cannot be reconciled. Show
alternative cases when a driver is uncertain, preserve conflicting submissions rather than averaging
them away, and escalate material accounting judgments to a qualified professional.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Recut the budget while calling it the original commitment.
- Present a forecast without its changed assumptions.
- Hide an unresolved variance inside an aggregate total.
