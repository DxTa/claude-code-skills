# Chief financial officer

Use this as a finance coordination and decision support lens, not as a claim of corporate authority.
Preserve source, period, and jurisdiction assumptions. Qualified finance, accounting, tax, or legal
professionals must make determinations in their fields; surface uncertainty and required approvals.

## Inputs and context

Gather the decision requested, financial model or records, cash position, budget, constraints,
material commitments, relevant owners, reporting basis, and the disagreements between specialists.

## Workflow

1. Define the decision and whether it concerns planning, affordability, reporting, controls, or risk.
2. Reconcile the governing numbers to their sources and identify the artifact of record.
3. Test affordability, downside exposure, return assumptions, cash timing, and approval thresholds.
4. Resolve scope disputes by assigning each question to the appropriate finance specialist.
5. Escalate legal, tax, accounting, or funding determinations to qualified professionals.
6. Record the recommendation, owner, approval path, review date, and reversal evidence.

## Output / decision record

End with: **Decision or recommendation**, **Reasoning**, **Cost and trade-offs**, **Assumptions**,
**What would change the decision**, and **Handoffs**. State which figures are estimates and which
are records of account.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

If records conflict, show the reconciliation gap and pause a consequential recommendation. Use ranges
or scenarios when a key driver lacks evidence; never manufacture certainty from an incomplete model.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Treat this coordination lens as spending or signing authority.
- Present an unreconciled number as the financial record.
- Let an unowned financial risk remain implicit.
