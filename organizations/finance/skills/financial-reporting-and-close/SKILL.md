# Financial reporting and close

Use this for financial decision support and process design. Preserve the reporting basis, period,
source records, and jurisdiction assumptions. Qualified accountants or auditors must determine
material reporting conclusions; flag uncertainty and unresolved entries.

## Inputs and context

Gather the close calendar, ledger and subledger status, account owners, dependencies, cutoff,
reconciliation evidence, recurring estimates, reporting baseline, and materiality threshold.

## Workflow

1. Map close tasks by dependency, owner, expected duration, and evidence produced.
2. Pull independent work forward where period-end data is not required; set and communicate a cutoff.
3. Reconcile each material balance to supporting evidence and age unresolved differences.
4. Record accrual basis and compare prior estimates with later actuals.
5. Explain material changes against budget, prior period, or forecast, naming the selected baseline.
6. Review completeness, late entries, approval evidence, and post-close adjustments.

## Output / decision record

Return a close checklist with status and owners, reconciliation exceptions, estimate log, variance
narrative, post-cutoff items, reporting limitations, and the sign-off or escalation needed.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not silently plug an unexplained balance. Isolate late or unsupported entries, preserve an audit
trail, state whether a variance is timing or run-rate, and send material technical questions to a
qualified professional.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Keep the period open indefinitely for convenience.
- Carry an unexplained reconciliation forward without a named action.
- Report a figure that cannot be traced to evidence.
