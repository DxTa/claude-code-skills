# Financial modeling

The result is financial decision support. Preserve the source, period, currency, accounting basis,
and jurisdiction assumptions. Qualified finance or accounting professionals must determine material
reporting treatment; label uncertainty instead of implying model precision.

## Inputs and context

Collect the decision question, historical baseline, forecast horizon, operational drivers, cost
behavior, cash timing, constraints, and source/date for every assumption. Separate target cases from
expected cases.

## Workflow

1. Define the unit, periods, scope, and outputs before building formulas.
2. Put assumptions in a visible input table; keep calculations separate from presentation outputs.
3. Build revenue and cost from operational drivers, distinguishing fixed, variable, and step changes.
4. Reconcile opening values and totals to source records and explain intentional differences.
5. Produce base, downside, and alternative cases; identify the few assumptions that move the answer.
6. Calculate break-even or decision-reversal points and review the model with its owner.

## Output / decision record

Return the model structure, assumption register, key outputs, sensitivity table, downside narrative,
limitations, and the decision the model supports. State precision no greater than the evidence allows.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Stop at an unexplained reconciliation break or circular dependency. Use ranges where inputs are weak,
show missing data, and escalate accounting, tax, or financing interpretation to a qualified adviser.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Hide assumptions inside unexplained formulas.
- Offer one scenario as a risk analysis.
- Change an input to reach a desired result without labeling a target case.
