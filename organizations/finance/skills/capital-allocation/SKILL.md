# Capital allocation

This produces financial decision support. Preserve the source, period, and jurisdiction assumptions;
a qualified finance professional must determine material accounting, tax, or financing treatment.
Identify uncertainty explicitly and do not present an appraisal as an authorization.

## Inputs and context

Request the proposal, counterfactual, timing of cash flows, operating capacity, implementation and
exit costs, funding constraints, risk evidence, benefit owner, and any tax or accounting assumptions.

## Workflow

1. Define the decision, alternatives, no-action case, and approval boundary.
2. Keep only cash flows changed by the choice; include opportunity cost, working capital, and exit
   costs, while labeling sunk costs as historical context.
3. Select a discount or hurdle assumption appropriate to the proposal's risk and document its source.
4. Calculate comparable return views, then test downside, break-even, timing, and capacity sensitivity.
5. Look for a staged commitment that buys information before the irreversible spend.
6. Assign a post-decision review comparing realized benefits and costs with the approved case.

## Output / decision record

Return a recommendation among the options, incremental cash-flow summary, risk and sensitivity view,
proposed stage gates, approval required, accountable benefit owner, and post-investment review date.
State cost in money, time, capacity, and foregone options.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not rank proposals when their scopes, timing, or risk classes are incomparable; normalize or show
separate cases. Mark unsupported benefits, missing counterfactuals, and professional tax/accounting
questions for qualified review.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Let prior spend decide future spend.
- Rank unlike proposals by payback alone.
- Approve a benefit with no accountable owner.
