# Corporate governance

This is governance decision support, not legal advice. Preserve entity, ownership, jurisdiction,
effective-date, document-version, and authority assumptions. Qualified counsel, corporate secretaries,
accountants, or insurers must determine legal or statutory requirements; record uncertainty.

## Inputs and context

Gather entity documents, cap table or ownership record, proposed decision, meeting notice, attendees,
conflicts, delegated authorities, contracts and insurance, retention policy, holds, and continuity
responsibilities. Process records only in an approved environment, minimize unrelated personal or
account data, mark confidential material, and retain it only for authorized need-to-know recipients.

## Workflow

1. Identify the entity, authority, decision type, required notice, quorum, conflicts, and approval path.
2. Separate items for decision, discussion, and information; prepare evidence before the meeting.
3. Draft a concise resolution or minutes record with participants, recusals, decision, rationale, and
   follow-up owner rather than inventing a transcript.
4. Check that delegation thresholds cover spending, contracts, hiring, and long-lived commitments.
5. Reconcile records, mark superseded versions, preserve holds, and test insurance and continuity
   contacts after material organizational change.

## Output / decision record

Return entity and authority assumptions, agenda or resolution draft, required approvals, decision and
minutes fields, record location/version, retention and hold status, continuity gaps, and qualified-
review questions.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not backfill an official record from memory when attendance, authority, or effective date is unclear.
Preserve superseded material, quarantine conflicting versions, and escalate statutory or jurisdictional
questions to qualified counsel.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Treat an undocumented threshold as authority.
- Delete a superseded record needed to explain past decisions.
- Present governance support as a legal determination.
