# Enterprise risk

This structures risk decision support, not legal, regulatory, insurance, or audit advice. Preserve
source evidence, assessment date, jurisdiction, organizational context, and scoring assumptions.
Qualified professionals must determine applicable requirements and material interpretations; mark
uncertainty and keep risk acceptance explicit.

## Inputs and context

Collect the decision or process, cause-and-consequence statement, evidence, existing controls, control
test results, likelihood and impact scales, risk appetite, owner, treatment options, and authority.

## Workflow

1. Write each risk as a plausible cause leading to a specific consequence.
2. Assess inherent exposure, current control effectiveness, residual exposure, and confidence separately.
3. Choose mitigate, transfer, avoid, or accept; quantify cost, dependencies, and target date.
4. Assign a named owner and route above-appetite risks to the authority that can accept them.
5. Gather evidence as work occurs, revisit stale ratings, and report only decisions and residual risks
   leadership must act on.

## Output / decision record

Return register entry, scoring rationale, evidence and confidence, controls, residual risk, treatment,
owners, deadline, approval or escalation, review cadence, and specific reversal evidence.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not lower a score merely because an item is old or a control exists. Mark untested controls,
missing evidence, correlated dependencies, and unresolved professional interpretations; keep the risk
open until a qualified reviewer or authorized owner decides.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Use a category label in place of a cause and consequence.
- Treat risk acceptance as implicit.
- Call a control effective without evidence of operation.
