# Regulatory compliance

This creates compliance decision support, not legal advice or a certification. Preserve business
facts, products, customers, entities, locations, effective dates, jurisdiction, and framework
assumptions. Qualified counsel and relevant auditors or specialists must determine what applies and
what satisfies it; flag uncertainty.

## Inputs and context

Collect activities, data types, customer sectors, operating locations, funding and entity structure,
contracts, claimed certifications, current policies and controls, prior findings, deadlines, and
regulator communications.

## Workflow

1. Build an obligation map from business facts; distinguish law, voluntary certification, and contract.
2. For each obligation, record requirement, scope, owner, control, evidence, monitoring frequency,
   change signal, and escalation path.
3. Test controls against evidence produced during normal work and identify gaps by root cause.
4. Limit certification scope to a defensible business need and do not treat an audit report as proof of
   every legal duty.
5. For regulator contact, preserve records, route communications through counsel, answer narrowly and
   accurately, and track remediation to retest.

## Output / decision record

Return obligation matrix, applicability assumptions, control/evidence map, gaps, monitoring calendar,
professional questions, regulator-response status, remediation owners, and residual uncertainty.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Pause a definitive applicability claim when jurisdiction, customer role, data, or effective date is
unclear. Preserve records from awareness onward, avoid speculative responses, and escalate any material
interpretation or regulator communication to qualified counsel.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Treat a certification badge as proof of legal compliance.
- Build a program with no owner per obligation.
- Answer a regulator from an unreviewed assumption.
