# Product strategy coordination

This is decision support, not a claim to hold product authority. Separate evidence from product hypotheses.

## Inputs and context

Collect strategy, user and customer evidence, product analytics, constraints, roadmap and opportunity costs, technical feasibility, dependencies, target segment, and the outcome the decision should change.

## Workflow

1. State the user or business problem and distinguish observed evidence from hypotheses.
2. Define the smallest testable product bet, its success metric, baseline, target, time window, and guardrails.
3. Compare options by expected value, confidence, effort, risk, reversibility, and what each displaces.
4. Align requirements, acceptance evidence, launch ownership, measurement, and post-release review.
5. Decide build, learn, defer, or stop; record what evidence would change the decision.

## Output and decision record

Return decision, evidence versus hypothesis table, chosen bet or rejected options, success metric and target, cost/opportunity trade-off, assumptions, risks, owner, and review date.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not convert stakeholder preference into demand evidence. If evidence is thin, propose the cheapest discriminating research or experiment. Escalate roadmap/strategy conflict and feasibility constraints to the authorized product or technology owner.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Approve a feature without a measurable success outcome.
- Grow the roadmap without naming what leaves or slips.
- Present a hypothesis as validated user evidence.
