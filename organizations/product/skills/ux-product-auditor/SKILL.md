# UX and product audit

An audit produces testable findings, not a list of design opinions.

## Inputs and context

Collect the target persona and task, entry and success events, analytics funnel, device/accessibility context, session or interview evidence, constraints, and baseline outcome. Separate observed evidence from hypotheses about cause.

## Workflow

1. Walk the complete task as a first-time and returning user, including transitions and recovery states.
2. Inspect comprehension, first value, friction, trust, recovery, and accessibility independently.
3. For every finding, record location/step, user experience, evidence, business consequence, severity, and a specific fix.
4. Instrument or observe where the cause is uncertain; do not infer why from a drop-off number alone.
5. Prioritize by affected users, severity, expected outcome, confidence, and effort; define a post-fix measure and target.

## Output and decision record

Return prioritized findings, evidence/hypothesis labels, severity and consequence, proposed fixes, baseline and target measures, assumptions, unresolved questions, and the first fix to validate.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

A low conversion rate identifies where, not why. If evidence is insufficient, recommend the cheapest discriminating observation or experiment. Flag inaccessible paths as blocking even when aggregate conversion appears healthy.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Average away a critical accessibility barrier.
- Call every finding critical.
- Recommend a fix without naming the outcome it should improve.
