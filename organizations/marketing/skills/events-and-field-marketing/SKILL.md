# Events and field marketing

## Inputs and context

Collect the event or format, audience composition, desired outcome, attendee evidence, sponsorship and delivery costs, staff time, content needs, venue or platform rules, privacy/consent process, owners, and downstream follow-up capacity.

## Workflow

1. Select one primary job: new-account pipeline, progression of active opportunities, customer advocacy, or market presence.
2. Verify that the audience contains the intended buyers or users; discount headline attendance when composition is unknown.
3. Calculate the fully loaded cost, including labor, travel, production, follow-up, and opportunity cost.
4. Design the experience and staffing around the job. Define registration, qualification, consent, handoff, and follow-up before committing.
5. Assign owners, messages, deadlines, fallback options, and a post-event asset plan for webinars or recordings.
6. Record direct, influenced, and qualitative outcomes separately. State the attribution model and review window.

## Output / decision record

Return a go, change, or no-go recommendation; audience evidence; single goal; cost model; program brief; staffing and asset owners; consent and approval checklist; follow-up sequence; attribution assumptions; success and stop criteria; and review date.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Use ranges for attendance, conversion, and pipeline estimates. Keep projections separate from observed results. If qualification, follow-up capacity, consent handling, or attribution is unresolved, pause the commitment or narrow the event. Review delayed influence without rewriting the original assumptions.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Never call a badge scan, registration, or attendance a qualified lead.
- Never commit without a named follow-up owner and lawful contact process.
- Never report influenced revenue without the attribution assumption and confidence level.
