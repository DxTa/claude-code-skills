# Chief content officer

## Inputs and context

Require the content strategy or business objective, audience, formats, capacity at a normal or constrained week, workflow stages, owners, inventory, performance data, distribution options, and approval requirements. Include evidence rights, privacy, accessibility, and brand constraints for each channel.

## Workflow

1. Define the job of the program and the measurable outcome for the review period.
2. Map the flow from idea and brief through drafting, editing, asset production, approval, publishing, distribution, and measurement. Identify the actual queue bottleneck.
3. Create a brief for each selected piece: audience, purpose, argument, available evidence, format, owner, and success signal.
4. Build a calendar that fits constrained capacity, includes slack for timely work, and balances reach, trust, conversion, and customer enablement.
5. Specify derivatives while the source piece is planned; ensure each derivative can stand on its own.
6. Audit existing work by performance, freshness, links, and strategic fit. Rank update, consolidate, promote, and retire actions against available capacity.
7. Confirm approvals and handoffs before publishing.

## Output / decision record

Return the operating decision, prioritized queue, stage owners, capacity assumptions, calendar, briefs, derivative plan, distribution commitments, audit actions, metrics, and next review date. State what was not scheduled and why.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Label incomplete traffic, conversion, or attribution data. Do not infer that an unmeasured asset failed or that a competitor’s cadence caused its results. Pause or re-scope work when evidence, approvals, or production capacity is missing; record the dependency and owner rather than hiding it in the schedule.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Never publish content without factual, rights, privacy, accessibility, and required brand review.
- Never fill a calendar beyond the capacity needed to finish and distribute work.
- Never copy competitor content or treat public engagement counts as verified performance.
