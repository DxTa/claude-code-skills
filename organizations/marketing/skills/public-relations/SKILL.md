# Public relations

## Inputs and context

Collect the proposed news, audience impact, verified evidence, timing, spokesperson, approved facts, journalist or outlet relevance, supporting assets and rights, confidentiality limits, response owners, and legal/security review requirements.

## Workflow

1. Test whether the story matters to the audience without relying on the company’s name. Look for a consequential fact, original evidence, meaningful change, or accountable person; if none exists, recommend research or no pitch.
2. Research relevant journalists and their recent work. Build a concise angle with why it matters now, why the spokesperson is qualified, and what verified material can be offered.
3. Prepare a short pitch or announcement with clear conditions, contact route, and follow-up limit. Obtain approvals before sending.
4. Brief the spokesperson on key truthful points, unknowns, expected questions, confidentiality, and the rule that “off the record” requires explicit agreement and is not a guarantee.
5. For a negative story, verify facts, state what happened, what is being done, and when the next update will arrive. Correct factual errors with specific evidence.
6. Log outreach, responses, coverage, corrections, and commitments without claiming causation from a single mention.

## Output / decision record

Return newsworthiness assessment, audience story, evidence ledger, target contacts and rationale, approved pitch or statement, spokesperson brief, risk and escalation plan, follow-up schedule, correction/update commitments, and outcome review.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Label unverified allegations, incomplete facts, embargo uncertainty, and likely audience interpretations. If facts cannot be checked, pause the statement and name the verifier. If no journalist fit exists, do not widen the list into spam; improve the story or use a different channel.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Never invent data, customers, exclusivity, quotes, or news value.
- Never send confidential, personal, or embargoed material without authorization and clear terms.
- Never attack a journalist or dispute an unchecked fact publicly.
