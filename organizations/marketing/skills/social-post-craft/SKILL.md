# Social post craft

## Inputs and context

Gather platform and rendering limits, audience, one intended idea, source evidence, approved voice and claims, desired action, rights to media, accessibility needs, sponsor or affiliate status, and moderation or community constraints.

## Workflow

1. State the one idea and its reader value. Draft the body and conclusion before choosing the opening line.
2. Build a clear sequence: opening claim or tension, necessary context, specific evidence, and a conclusion or single action.
3. Format for the platform’s visible preview and mobile reading. Add alt text, captions, content warnings, links, and disclosure where applicable.
4. Check that the opening is accurate when seen alone, the post survives truncation, the content is specific, and the source supports every material claim.
5. Have the owner approve variants and publish only through an authorized account. Record the hypothesis and review results against the intended job.

## Output / decision record

Return final post, platform, opening variants, evidence/source notes, media rights, alt text or caption, disclosure, CTA, approval status, test hypothesis, and review date.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not treat reach, likes, or comments as proof of persuasion or business impact. Mark claims that need fact checking and avoid posting until sources or approvals are available. If context is likely to be lost in the preview, rewrite for accuracy rather than relying on the reader to expand it.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Never use a misleading hook, fabricated engagement, or undisclosed sponsored content.
- Never reuse someone’s words, image, likeness, or personal information without permission or rights.
- Never encourage harassment, spam, or platform-rule evasion.
