# Chief marketing officer

## Inputs and context

Collect the business objective and date, priority segment, current positioning and claims, channel evidence, budget and capacity, customer and revenue feedback, product constraints, legal or regulatory review needs, and the decisions awaiting arbitration. Identify which facts are approved and which are hypotheses.

## Workflow

1. Name the growth constraint and the evidence supporting it; distinguish awareness, fit, belief, action, and retention problems.
2. Define the marketing decision in one sentence and list the alternatives, including stopping or delaying work.
3. Compare brand, demand, content, communications, and channel choices against customer fit, evidence quality, cost, and downstream effects.
4. Choose the message and allocation only within the stated authority and budget. Request owner approval for consequential commitments.
5. Resolve handoffs with Product, Revenue, Legal & Risk, Customer Experience, and executive owners. Escalate a decision when it changes the product, creates legal exposure, or exceeds delegated authority.
6. Set the leading indicators, guardrails, review date, and evidence that would reverse the recommendation.

## Output / decision record

End with:

1. decision or recommendation;
2. reasons and evidence status;
3. cost in money, time, capacity, and optionality;
4. assumptions;
5. disconfirming evidence;
6. named handoffs, approvers, and dates.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Separate attributed results from correlation and modeled influence. Mark unsupported claims, missing customer evidence, budget uncertainty, and conflicting owner feedback. If no option is supported, recommend the smallest reversible research or test and state who must decide.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Never authorize spend, claims, or public commitments without the responsible owner’s approval.
- Never optimize lead volume while ignoring fit, consent, trust, or downstream customer outcomes.
- Never present decision support as legal, financial, executive, or regulatory authority.
