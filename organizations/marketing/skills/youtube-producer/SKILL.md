# YouTube producer

## Inputs and context

Gather the channel promise and audience, topic demand evidence, creator expertise, existing catalog, production capacity, intended viewer outcome, runtime, packaging constraints, source material, sponsor details, rights, accessibility, and platform rules.

## Workflow

1. Select ideas where audience need, distinctive knowledge, and an honest clickable premise overlap. Review existing coverage to find an informed angle rather than copying it.
2. Create three title/thumbnail packages. Make each specific, readable at phone size, complementary, and deliverable by the video.
3. Design the opening around immediate value and a clear map of the commitment. Remove channel introductions that delay the substance.
4. Outline chapters with a reason to continue at each transition, front-loaded value, deliberate pacing, and a sponsor placement that is disclosed and not misleading.
5. Add visual direction, on-screen text, b-roll purpose, cut points, and captions to the production plan. Identify segments that can stand alone.
6. Define topic, packaging, opening, and retention hypotheses with comparable baseline metrics. Review channel-level consistency before scheduling.

## Output / decision record

Return idea and evidence, channel fit, three packages and selection, viewer promise, opening, chapter outline, production/edit notes, rights and disclosure checklist, derivative plan, success measures, risks, owner, and review date.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Treat search demand, click-through, retention, and growth forecasts as uncertain. Diagnose low clicks, early exits, transition cliffs, and weak distribution separately using timestamps and channel baselines. If a package cannot be fulfilled or evidence is thin, narrow the premise or defer production.

## Never

- Never copy a competitor’s script, package, footage, or distinctive expression.
- Never use a title or thumbnail that misrepresents the video.
- Never hide sponsors, use rightsless assets, or state unsupported results as facts.
