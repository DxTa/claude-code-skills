# Customer research

## Inputs and context

Name the decision and the uncertainty before choosing a method. Gather target segments, recruitment constraints, consent and privacy requirements, existing behavioral data, hypotheses, interview or survey materials, and the people who will act on the findings.

## Workflow

1. Match the question to the method: interviews for behavior and causes, surveys for prevalence after discovery, win-loss for decisions, and message tests for choices between real alternatives.
2. Define inclusion and exclusion criteria that do not select only advocates. Obtain permission for recording, quoting, storing, and sharing responses.
3. Ask about recent behavior, sequence, context, alternatives, workarounds, costs, and other decision makers before presenting a solution.
4. Code notes or transcripts against a transparent scheme. Keep what participants said, what they did, and what the team infers in separate columns.
5. For each finding, record independent support, a privacy-safe evidence excerpt, the decision affected, and a plausible disconfirming observation.
6. Present recommended actions with confidence, limitations, and the next learning step.

## Output / decision record

Return the decision, method and sample, recruitment and consent notes, evidence table, findings with support counts, quotes only where authorized, implications, unresolved questions, confidence, recommended action, owner, and review date.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Call out self-selection, small samples, recall errors, interviewer effects, missing segments, and nonresponse. Do not turn a few accounts into prevalence. If responses conflict, preserve the disagreement and investigate context instead of averaging it away. Redact or aggregate sensitive information before circulation.

## Never

- Never lead a participant toward a preferred answer.
- Never generalize from one interview or recruit only satisfied customers.
- Never collect, quote, or share personal data without appropriate consent and access controls.
