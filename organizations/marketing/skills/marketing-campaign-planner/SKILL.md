# Marketing campaign planner

## Inputs and context

Gather launch or campaign dates, audience, approved positioning, offer, primary objective, leading indicator, channel options, budget, assets, owners, dependencies, review gates, platform rules, and delivery/support capacity.

## Workflow

1. State the campaign story as one sentence: audience, change, and reason to act now. If the story needs multiple unrelated claims, split the work.
2. Set one primary outcome with a date, one early signal, and explicit non-goals.
3. Select a small channel set by audience access, evidence, production ability, and risk. Sequence owned, earned, partner, or paid activity according to readiness rather than running everything simultaneously.
4. Build an asset matrix with message, format, owner, due date, rights, and approval for each channel.
5. Work backward from launch, place a freeze before release, and schedule the learning and follow-up period afterward.
6. Define the largest failure mode, a fallback, budget guardrails, and conditions for pause or expansion.
7. Verify offer terms, claims, consent, accessibility, and operational capacity before launch.

## Output / decision record

Return the one-sentence story, objective and leading indicator, non-goals, channel sequence and rationale, timeline and freeze date, asset/owner matrix, dependencies, risk/fallback table, approvals, measurement plan, and post-launch review date.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Label forecast assumptions and distinguish planned from approved assets. Use scenarios for half-budget, delayed dependency, and higher-than-expected demand. If proof, offer terms, legal review, or fulfillment capacity is missing, recommend a smaller test or delay instead of compensating with urgency.

## Never

- Never ship unapproved claims, pricing, targeting, or customer data collection.
- Never manufacture urgency, scarcity, testimonials, or social proof.
- Never launch with no owner for follow-up, support, or measurement.
