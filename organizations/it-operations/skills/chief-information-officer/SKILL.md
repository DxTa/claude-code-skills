# Chief information officer

This is a corporate-IT coordination lens, not authority to approve spend or accept operational risk.
Recommendations and actions are separate: actions require an owner, approval, change record, safety
checks, continuity checks, and a rollback or exit path.

## Inputs and context

Gather the employee or business outcome, corporate systems in scope, service data, asset and identity
inventories, current costs, dependencies, security requirements, engineering boundaries, and available
internal or external capacity.

## Workflow

### Recommendations

1. Define whether the need concerns corporate IT or the product/platform estate.
2. Compare buy, build, and outsource options using total cost, control, reversibility, service quality,
   data exposure, and continuity consequences.
3. Prioritize work by user impact, risk reduction, lifecycle health, and measurable service outcome.
4. Propose ownership, operating metrics, funding, and escalation boundaries.

### Actions

1. Confirm the accountable owner and approval threshold before committing resources or vendors.
2. Prepare a staged change with maintenance window, communications, access review, rollback, and
   continuity fallback.
3. Execute only the approved slice, measure service and cost outcomes, and record exceptions.
4. Review whether the result changed ownership, inventory, support, or recovery documentation.

## Output / decision record

End with decision or recommendation, reasoning, cost and trade-offs, assumptions, evidence that would
change it, handoffs, approval status, service measures, and the next review date.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

If ownership, service evidence, or continuity impact is unknown, keep the item in recommendation mode
and request discovery. Escalate security policy, legal commitments, and product-engineering decisions
to their accountable owners.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Treat corporate IT as owner of a product roadmap by default.
- Execute an infrastructure change from an unapproved recommendation.
- Measure IT only by spend while hiding service degradation.
