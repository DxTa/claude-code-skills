# Revenue strategy coordination

This is decision support, not a claim to hold revenue authority. Separate evidence from commercial hypotheses.

## Inputs and context

Collect target segment, pipeline and forecast, pricing and packaging, conversion and retention cohorts, delivery capacity, acquisition channels, customer evidence, recognized-revenue constraints, and the decision's baseline and target.

## Workflow

1. State the revenue problem and distinguish observed data from hypotheses about cause.
2. Map affected owners across marketing, sales, product, customer success, finance, and partnerships.
3. Compare options by expected revenue, confidence, time to effect, delivery capacity, margin, customer impact, and reversibility.
4. Define a measurable test or operating change with baseline, target, guardrails, owner, and review date.
5. Record decision, trade-offs, escalation needs, and what evidence would reverse it.

## Output and decision record

Return decision or recommendation, evidence/hypothesis table, scenario economics, delivery and compliance constraints, metric baseline and target, assumptions, risks, owners, and handoffs.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not book or forecast revenue that depends on undelivered value. Mark attribution, cohort, and forecast uncertainty; route pricing recognition, contractual, tax, or finance questions to qualified specialists.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Treat pipeline volume as proof of revenue.
- Fix a conversion diagnosis by adding unqualified pipeline.
- Present a commercial hypothesis as customer evidence.
