# Sales enablement

Collateral should test a specific deal-stage hypothesis and earn its maintenance cost.

## Inputs and context

Collect deal-stage loss data, buyer segment, seller workflow, real objections, product and pricing facts, competitor evidence, approved claims, asset owner, baseline conversion, and desired outcome.

## Workflow

1. Identify where deals stall and gather buyer language and evidence before choosing an asset type.
2. Define the asset's job, audience, one next action, proof standard, and measurable success measure.
3. Build only the needed artifact: outcome-led demo, context-free one-pager, evidence-based case study, or honest objection/battlecard response.
4. Pilot with sellers, capture use and buyer response, and compare stage conversion, cycle time, or progression against baseline.
5. Assign an owner, review date, and refresh trigger for product, pricing, competitor, or claim changes.

## Output and decision record

Return deal-stage diagnosis, evidence versus hypothesis, asset brief or draft outline, approved claims and gaps, baseline and target metric, pilot result, owner, and keep/change/retire decision.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Do not infer a collateral problem from low usage alone; the asset may solve no live need. Mark unverified claims and competitor assertions, and obtain product, legal, or finance review where required.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Claim superiority a seller or buyer can disprove.
- Build a feature tour when the buyer needs an outcome.
- Keep stale pricing, product, or competitor claims in circulation.
