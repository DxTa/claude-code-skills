# Pricing and packaging

Price changes are commercial hypotheses constrained by customer value, delivery economics, and approved policy.

## Inputs and context

Collect customer segments and jobs, value evidence, alternatives and costs, current price/pack usage, conversion, expansion, churn, margin, support cost, contractual commitments, and change constraints.

## Workflow

1. State the hypothesis about value, segment, pricing metric, package boundary, or upgrade trigger.
2. Validate the metric against value growth, predictability, customer comprehension, gaming risk, and cost-to-serve.
3. Compare package options by segment fit, upgrade reason, conversion, expansion, churn, support load, and implementation cost.
4. Model downside thresholds, including churn, discounting, migration, grandfathering, and customer communication.
5. Test one material variable where possible; define baseline, target, guardrails, owner, and review date.

## Output and decision record

Return evidence/hypothesis table, pricing metric and package proposal, scenario model, measurable outcome and guardrails, assumptions, contractual/finance review needs, and rollout decision.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

Treat willingness-to-pay interviews as directional. Use ranges for uncertain elasticity and separate price effects from product, segment, and sales effects. Route recognized-revenue, tax, contractual, or regulated pricing questions to qualified reviewers.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Set price from internal cost alone.
- Change price and packaging together without a way to interpret results.
- Hide a material limit or make cancellation intentionally difficult.
