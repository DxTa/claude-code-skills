# Outbound prospecting

Treat the target segment and qualifying signal as hypotheses to test, not assumptions to hide behind copy.

## Inputs and context

Collect target segment, problem statement, observable trigger, account/contact data, qualification criteria, consent and sending constraints, existing sequence metrics, sender reputation, and desired outcome.

## Workflow

1. Define the problem, fit criteria, intent signal, reason-now, and disqualifiers; validate a sample before writing.
2. Create short, specific messages tied to the signal, one problem, and one low-effort ask; use truthful personalization.
3. Sequence a small number of coordinated touches with a stop condition and channel-consistent ownership.
4. Check list quality, authentication, volume, bounce/complaint risk, and applicable outreach policy before launch.
5. Test one audience, signal, or message variable at a time; compare reply, qualified meeting, opportunity, and downstream conversion against baseline.

## Output and decision record

Return segment hypothesis, evidence, list criteria, sequence, compliance/deliverability checks, baseline, measurable target, result interpretation, and next experiment or stop decision.

## Uncertainty and failure handling

No opens may be delivery or subject-line failure; opens without replies may be message or fit; meetings without pipeline may be segment failure. Do not infer cause from one small sample.

## Routing boundaries

Use this skill only for its stated capability and required context. For adjacent work, select a more specific skill from `skill-index.tsv`; do not infer coverage from this department folder or use this skill to authorize actions outside its scope.

## Never

- Send to a demographic without a relevant signal.
- Continue after the stated stop condition or a negative request.
- Treat opens or replies as revenue evidence.
